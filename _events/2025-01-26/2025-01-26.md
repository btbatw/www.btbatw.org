Speaker Intro:

Dr. Yen-Po (Harvey) Chin, MD, PhD, is the co-founder and chairman of AGM International Group, a management consulting firm helping public and private companies grow internationally. 

He formerly held the position of management consultant at McKinsey & Company, serving a diverse client base in the life science (biotech/medtech/pharma/CDMO) and technology (AI/SaaS) sectors. His experience spans business development and licensing strategies, cross-border market entry strategies, B2B sales strategies, as well as M&A/IPO/fundraising strategies. Furthermore, he has supported leading private equity firms on due diligence, portfolio growth, and exit strategies.

Prior to McKinsey, Dr. Harvey was an inaugural Harvard Healthtech Innovation Fellow, where he worked alongside physicians and scientists from Harvard to turn scientific innovations into fundable business ventures. Preceding this, he established himself as a serial entrepreneur in the medical AI domain, earning recognition as a Forbes 30 Under 30 honoree—the first Taiwanese physician to receive the award. Before that, he was a physician at Taipei Medical University Hospital. 

Dr. Harvey received his MD from National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, a PhD in Biomedical Informatics from Taipei Medical University, and a postdoctoral master's degree in Biomedical Informatics from Harvard Medical School.

In addition to his professional achievements, Harvey is also the co-founder of AAC talent hub and is committed to mentoring and coaching talented individuals, having successfully guided over 700 students and young professionals in realizing their career aspirations to date.
